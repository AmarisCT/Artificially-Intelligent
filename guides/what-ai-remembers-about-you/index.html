What AI assistants remember about you, and how to see or delete it

Most AI assistants now keep a running memory of the people who use them. Here is what six of them keep, where to find it, how to delete it, and what can stay behind after you do.

The short version

  • Our readingMemory, chat history and training are three different switches. Turning one off rarely turns off the others. Memory is what the assistant knows about you; history is the list of your chats; training is whether your chats help build future models.
  • From the sourceMemory can be on before you ever touch a setting. Claude's memory, for example, is on by default on its Free, Pro and Max plans.[6]
  • From the sourceDeleting a chat doesn't always delete what was learned from it. On Claude you have to delete the memory too.[6] On Gemini, deleting the chat is how you stop it shaping answers.[8]
  • From the source“Private” chats are still kept for a while. 30 days for ChatGPT's Temporary Chat[1] and Grok's Private Chat,[15] 72 hours for Gemini's temporary chats.[8]
  • From the sourceReportedSome copies outlive your delete button. Gemini chats read by human reviewers are kept for up to three years,[8] and Meta uses Meta AI chats to personalize ads outside the EU, UK and South Korea.[14]

Assistant by assistant

ChatGPT

OpenAI

What it remembers

ReportedDetails and preferences you share, plus what it picks up from your past chats. Since June 2026 a system OpenAI calls “dreaming” updates this memory in the background and shows it as a memory summary you can edit. It reached Plus and Pro users in the US first.[4]

See it

ReportedSettings → Personalization → Memory, where the memory summary lives.[3][4]

From the sourceYou can also simply ask ChatGPT what it remembers about you.[2]

Delete it or turn it off

ReportedFrom the memory summary, the menu option “Delete and turn off memory” removes what is stored and switches memory off. It does not delete your past chats. Turning off “Reference chat history” schedules what was learned from past chats for deletion within 30 days.[3]

Chat without memory

From the sourceTemporary Chat doesn't use or create memories, isn't saved to your history and isn't used for training. It is deleted from OpenAI's systems after 30 days.[1]

Training

From the sourceSettings → Data Controls → Improve the model for everyone: switch it off. Your chat history stays; only the training use stops.[1]

What can stay after you delete

ReportedOpenAI may keep logs of deleted memories for up to 30 days.[3]

ReportedIn 2025 a court order in the New York Times lawsuit made OpenAI keep deleted chats. That ended for new chats from September 26, 2025, except for accounts the Times flagged.[5]

Last checked October 29, 2026.

Claude

Anthropic

What it remembers

From the sourceContext from your chats, stored as topics. Memory is on by default on the Free, Pro and Max plans. Sensitive topics aren't saved unless you switch on “Include sensitive topics in memory.” Each project keeps its own memory.[6]

See it

From the sourceSettings → Your Claude, then “Manage” next to Your memory. Open a topic to read it, edit it or delete it. You can also tell Claude what to remember or forget.[6]

Delete it or turn it off

From the sourceSwitch off “Memory from chats” in the same place, then choose “Pause memory” (keeps what's stored but stops using it) or “Reset memory” (permanently deletes all memories, including project memories).[6]

Chat without memory

From the sourceIncognito chat, the ghost icon at the top right, isn't saved to your history or memory. To skip memory for one chat only, turn off “Memory” under “+” before your first message.[6]

Training

From the sourceChoose in your Privacy settings whether your chats can be used to train models. If you allow it, Anthropic keeps that data for up to five years; if not, the 30-day period applies. Deleted conversations aren't used for future training.[7]

What can stay after you delete

From the sourceDeleting a conversation does not remove memories created from it. Delete those memories separately.[6]

Last checked October 29, 2026.

Gemini

Google

What it remembers

From the sourceWith Memory on, Gemini can draw on the chats saved in your Gemini Apps Activity, including sensitive things you shared. It also keeps “Instructions for Gemini” (called Saved Info in some places) until you delete them.[8]

See it

From the sourceAsk “What do you know about me?”, or view and edit the memory summary in Gemini's settings. Your saved chats are listed at myactivity.google.com/product/gemini.[8]

Delete it or turn it off

From the sourceDelete a chat to stop it shaping Gemini's personalization. Turning off “Keep Activity” stops saving future chats. Saved activity auto-deletes after 18 months by default; you can pick 3 or 36 months, or no auto-delete.[8]

Chat without memory

From the sourceTemporary chats aren't used to train Google's models or reviewed by people, but they are still kept with your account for 72 hours. The same applies to chats made with Keep Activity off.[8]

Training

From the sourceWith Keep Activity off, future chats aren't used to train Google's models unless you send feedback on a chat.[8]

What can stay after you delete

From the sourceA sample of chats is read by human reviewers. Reviewed chats are kept for up to three years, disconnected from your account, even if you delete your activity.[8]

Last checked October 29, 2026.

Copilot

Microsoft

What it remembers

From the sourceDetails such as your communication style, favorite topics, goals and recurring tasks. This applies to personal Microsoft accounts, not work or school accounts.[9]

See it

From the sourceSettings → Personalization, then “Manage” next to Saved memories. You can also ask Copilot “What do you know about me?”[9][11]

Delete it or turn it off

From the sourceDelete one memory with the trash icon, or choose “Delete all memories.” Turning off the “Saved memories” switch stops new memories but does not delete existing ones.[9]

Chat history

From the sourceCopilot keeps conversation history for 18 months by default. You can delete single conversations or all of it.[10]

Training

From the sourceYou can opt out of training on your conversations. In the earlier Copilot app the switches are “Training on conversation activity” and “Training on voice conversations” under Privacy. Opting out applies to future conversations.[10][11]

Last checked October 29, 2026.

Meta AI

Meta · Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger

What it remembers

From the sourceMeta AI can remember details about you from your chats. Meta's help page explains how to delete them.[12]

Delete your chats

ReportedIn Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp, type /reset-ai in an AI chat to delete Meta's copy of that conversation, or /reset-all-ais for every AI chat in that app. On WhatsApp, do this before deleting the thread, because deleting the thread only removes your own copy.[13]

In the Meta AI app

ReportedSettings → Data & privacy → Manage your information, then “Delete all chats and media,” which covers the app and meta.ai on the web.[13]

Ads

ReportedSince December 16, 2025, Meta uses your conversations with Meta AI to personalize ads and posts on its apps. Meta said there is no opt-out. It doesn't apply in the EU, UK or South Korea, and Meta says chats about religion, sexual orientation, politics, health, or racial or ethnic origin aren't used for ads.[14]

Last checked October 29, 2026.

Grok

xAI

What it remembers

ReportedGrok can remember past conversations to personalize answers. When memory launched in April 2025, xAI said you could see what it remembers, delete single memories in the chat, and turn the feature off in privacy settings. It wasn't offered in the EU or UK at launch.[16]

Check the details yourself

From the sourcexAI's public FAQ (last updated May 2025) doesn't explain memory, so check your own Grok settings.[15]

Delete your chats

From the sourceIn the app, Settings → Data Controls → Delete All Conversations. Removal from xAI's systems can take up to 30 days.[15]

Chat without memory

From the sourcePrivate Chat, the ghost icon at the top right: it won't appear in your history, it is deleted within 30 days, and it isn't used for training.[15]

Training

From the sourceApp: Settings → Data Controls → Improve the model. Grok.com: Settings → Data → Improve the Model. Feedback you send can still be used.[15]

Last checked October 29, 2026.

A ten-minute check

Our reading Our suggestions, not the companies'.

  1. Ask first. In each assistant you use, ask “What do you remember about me?” The answer shows you what it would bring into your next chat.
  2. Open the memory page and prune it. Delete anything you wouldn't want said back to you, or seen by someone else on your device.
  3. Decide on training separately. It's a different switch from memory. Turning memory off doesn't stop training, and the reverse is also true.
  4. Use private chats for sensitive questions, and remember they're still kept for a short time.
  5. Clear out old chats you don't need. On Claude, delete any memories they created as well.
  6. Keep a short “never tell a chatbot” list: passwords, ID and account numbers, and anything about other people that they didn't agree to share.

Why this matters

Our readingIn We Are All Archives Now, we followed how records made for everyday needs, such as a barley account in clay or a land survey, outlived the moment that produced them. AI memory is the newest version of that story. A preference you mention once can become part of a lasting profile, kept by a company, used to shape what you are shown, and harder to see than a box of letters in a drawer.

SpeculationAn open question for this archive: when an assistant's memory of you shapes how it answers you, how much of that “you” is a record, and how much is an interpretation someone else made?
