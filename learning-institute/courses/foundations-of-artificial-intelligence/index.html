Foundations of Artificial Intelligence

How do artificial intelligence systems learn, reason, and generate responses?

A first course on what AI is, how machine learning and neural networks work, how language models generate text, and how AI systems are evaluated. It does not treat all AI systems as the same kind of machine.

About 1.3 hours across 8 lessons. Version 1.0.0, last reviewed 2026-10-16. Review status: Awaiting editorial review.

Learning objectives

  • Explain what the term artificial intelligence covers and what it leaves out.
  • Describe how a machine learning system learns from examples.
  • Explain at a basic level how neural networks and language models work.
  • Describe how AI capabilities are evaluated and why evaluation is hard.
  • Distinguish intelligence-like behaviour from consciousness, and name limitations and risks.

Lessons

  1. What artificial intelligence means
  2. A brief history of AI research
  3. Machine learning fundamentals
  4. Neural networks
  5. Language models
  6. Reasoning and evaluation
  7. Intelligence versus consciousness
  8. Limitations, risks, and open questions
