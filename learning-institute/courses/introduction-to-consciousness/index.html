Introduction to Consciousness

What does it mean to be conscious?

A first course on what the word consciousness covers, why subjective experience is hard to study, and how scientists and philosophers approach it. It treats open questions as open.

About 1.4 hours across 8 lessons. Version 1.0.0, last reviewed 2026-10-16. Review status: Awaiting editorial review.

Learning objectives

  • Explain several important meanings of consciousness.
  • Distinguish subjective experience from related cognitive functions.
  • Identify significant approaches to studying consciousness.
  • Recognize major unresolved questions.
  • Explain why consciousness remains an active area of scientific and philosophical investigation.

Lessons

  1. Defining consciousness
  2. Subjective experience and awareness
  3. Consciousness and perception
  4. Consciousness and attention
  5. Neural correlates of consciousness
  6. Major scientific and philosophical theories
  7. The limits of current knowledge
  8. Final research reflection
